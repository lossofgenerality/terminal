Welcome

This manual shows SFX Ray staff how to use the apps provided by Computational Classnotes (CCN). Each chapter is for one kind of person, so read only the chapters you need. Every app works on a phone, a tablet or a computer.

The pictures were taken in each app's test mode, with made-up data. Your own screens show your own jobs, people and pages.

Everyone

Your key

Each person signs in with their own key. Workers have a passkey, supervisors an author key and managers a manager key. For CCN Camera, the company has a customer ID and key.

  • Keep it to yourself. Never write it on the station or send it in a message. CCN never asks you for it.
  • Spaces and quotation marks around a key do not matter, so a key pasted with its quotes still works.
  • You type your key once, at sign-in. After that the app uses a short-lived sign-in of its own, which ends when you sign out.
  • Lost or shared a key? Tell your manager, and a new one can be made.

The one rule to remember

When the system has received what you did, you see Confirmed in green beside it. No Confirmed? Press again. If you see Press again! in full red, press that box once more.

Workers

On the floor: the Terminal

One job per sign-in. You tick each step as you finish it, and sign out when every step is done.

The first time on a station

  1. Install the app and name the station Press Install app and give the station its name, for example Packing-12. The name then shows in the title. This is done once per device, usually by your supervisor.

Every job

  1. Sign in Type your passkey and press Sign in.
  2. Choose your job Pick it from the Job list. Its instructions appear underneath: read them first. Then its steps appear, each with its number.
  3. Tick each step as you finish it Tap the box beside the step. Confirmed appears beside it within a moment.
  4. Saw Press again!? If the connection is lost for a moment, the tick goes back after about 5 seconds and Press again! appears. Simply tap the box again.
  5. Sign out When every step shows Confirmed, Sign out becomes available. Press it, then Yes, sign out. The job is recorded as done, and the next person sees one job fewer.

Message your manager

Type in Write your message and press Send. This works even if you cannot sign in. A small dot shows what happened to the message:

  • red: not sent; press Send again
  • yellow: delivered, not yet read
  • green: your manager has opened it
  • blue: handled

Not every message needs a written reply. For "come and see me", your manager simply comes.

Emergency

In an accident, press the red Emergency button once. Anyone can press it, signed in or not. If it cannot be sent, the page says so and keeps trying: raise the alarm on site at once, as you normally would.

Supervisors

Writing jobs: Job authoring

The jobs you write here appear on every Terminal. The steps you set are the boxes the workers tick.

  1. Sign in with your author key
  2. Open a job, or press New job Give it a short Job ID (for example MI) and a name (for example Morning inspection).
  3. Write the instructions The Job specifics box works like a word processor. Select text, then press B for bold, I for italic, a colour dot to colour it, or List for bullet points. Clear removes the formatting.
  4. Add the steps Each step has an ID (for example MI-01) and a short text. Keep steps short and in the order they are done.
  5. Check the preview, then Save The preview shows the job exactly as a worker will see it. Press Save. The job is on every Terminal at the next sign-in.

Managers

Managing the floor: the Journal

Everything the Terminals report, in one place: who is working where, how far along they are, and where the work slows down. Sign in with your manager key.

Live: the floor right now

It refreshes every minute. At the top are today's figures. Below, a card for each work-table shows who is signed in, the job, how far along it is, and how long the current step has taken. Running long (in amber) means a step is taking more than one and a half times its usual time.

Bottlenecks: where the work slows down

Choose the dates, a group and a job. The heatmap shows each work-table, hour by hour. Amber means slower than usual for those steps. Below it are the slowest steps, the groups compared, and trouble signs such as unticks and Press again!. Download CSV gives you the figures in a spreadsheet.

Timeline, Drill-down and Groups

  • Timeline: one day at every work-table. Jobs are bars, and steps running long are amber.
  • Drill-down: one job by one person. Time per step, and every click as the system received it.
  • Groups: make your own groups of work-tables and people, such as First floor, Cutters or Second shift. A table or person can be in several groups.

Whoever makes witness pages

Witness pages: the CCN Camera builder

Turn photos, a video or documents (PDF, text, Excel) into a witness page. Every item is read: text in any language, names, numbers, codes and more. Each item gets its own address.

  1. Sign in Choose Customer, type your customer ID and key, and press Check connection. The page lists what your account includes.
  2. Add your files and choose the options Drop in or choose photos, a video or documents; you can add more at any time. Leave the title empty to have one suggested. The builder shows the estimated cost before you start.
  3. Build, check, publish Press Build witness page. Check the preview, then Publish to the web (or Download ZIP, where your account includes it).
  4. Your published pages Under My published pages, press Show the pages to see them all, newest first. Copy makes a duplicate under a new address without reading anything again. Delete removes a page once you have typed its name.

Reading a witness page

Open the page's address. Tap any item to see what was read. Use Search to find text, names or numbers. Copy link gives the address of one exact item, for example #F03. Where your account includes them, there is a support assistant to ask questions, and an Install button.

Readings are made by AI and may contain mistakes. Check anything important against the photo, video or document itself.

The account holder

Your account: the CCN Camera admin

Keep your company's details up to date, choose which parts of the service you use, and see your usage.

  1. Sign in as Customer Choose Customer, then type your customer ID and key.
  2. Business details Your contact email; a privacy contact, for anyone who objects to appearing in your media; how long records are kept; and the assistant's languages. Press Save.
  3. Parts of the service Switch off anything you do not want. Greyed-out parts are not in your plan: contact CCN to add them.
  4. Your limits and usage These show what your plan allows each month and what you have used so far. Your published pages lists your pages, as in the builder.

Everyone

Good to know

Privacy

  • While you are testing, your witness pages can be opened by anyone who has the address. Once you approve the software, your pages become private.
  • People who appear in your photos and videos are personal data. Faces are described, never identified. Your privacy contact (in the admin) receives any objections.
  • The Terminal records each click with its time, station and device. That is how the Journal works. Your company tells staff what is recorded and for how long.

If something goes wrong

  • The passkey is not accepted: check it, then use Message to tell your manager.
  • Press again! keeps coming back: the station has no connection. Check the Wi-Fi; your ticks are kept until it returns.
  • Sign out stays grey: a step is not yet ticked, or not yet Confirmed.
  • An app looks out of date: reload the page.
  • Anything else: [SUPPORT CONTACT]

Station, work-table
The place, and its device, where the work is done, for example Packing-12.
Confirmed
The system has received what you did.
Press again!
It did not arrive: press once more.
Group
Any set of work-tables and people that a manager chooses, for comparing.
CCN Camera
The service that turns your photos, video or documents into witness pages: the builder, the pages and the admin.
Witness page
A web page made with CCN Camera from your photos, video or documents, with every item read.
